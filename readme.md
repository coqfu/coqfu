## 🧠 ML Research & Engineering (Project NOVA)

I am transitioning from small-model engineering into serious language-model research. My biggest strength is an **experimental and debugging mindset**. Instead of just tweaking learning rates or adding data blindly, I investigate root causes.

*   **Numerical Debugging:** Identifying fast-memory explosions, optimizer instability, and checkpoint corruption bugs.
*   **Rigorous Validation:** Rejecting "fake progress." A low training loss isn't enough; I track true validation improvements, semantic generalization, and verify exact checkpoints.
*   **Custom Architecture:** Optimizing CPU/native hot-paths, staging training phases, and designing controlled experiments (e.g., fast-memory coupling ablation).
*   **Current Growth Areas:** Dataset engineering (100M+ token pipelines), distributed training logic, and scaling laws.
